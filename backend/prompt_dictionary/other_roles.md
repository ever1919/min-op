Summarize the RELEVANT EXPERIENCE into up to 9 roles, based strictly on the candidate CV.

Formatting and structure:
- Each role must have this structure:
    Context / Client Descriptor – **Program or Initiative – Role Name**
    Role description paragraph

- The part enclosed in ** ** must always be the Program or Initiative and Role Name.
- The ** ** markers are mandatory in every role title.
- Do not bold the context / client descriptor.
- Use an en dash (–) between context and role.

Role description rules:
- Write exactly one paragraph per role.
- The paragraph must not exceed the length of the following reference text:
  "Responsible for defining and driving the overall test strategy, managing the QA team, and coordinating cross-vendor and cross-function testing activities. Oversees test planning and execution, ensures defect and risk management, and communicates project status to stakeholders, ensuring alignment with project goals."
- Focus on responsibilities, scope, leadership, and impact.
- Bold **keywords**, **skills**, or **methodologies** only.
- Do not use bullet points, dashes, or numbering.
- Exclude company names, institutions, locations, and dates.

Content constraints:
- Maximum of 9 roles.
- If fewer roles exist, return only those supported by the CV.
- Do not invent roles, responsibilities, or achievements.
- Do not repeat the same role with minor variations.

Output rules:
- Separate each role block with a single blank line.
- Do not add headers, labels, or explanations.
- Output roles only.
